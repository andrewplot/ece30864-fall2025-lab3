a: Pros of Agile: fast, adaptive, and fosters collaboration

Cons: Can be chaotic and directionless if not implemented correctly

b: 
Scrum master is the coach for the development team, focusing on the process and team effectiveness. Product owner is more of a creative role, being the voice of the customer and responsible for defining features and aligning with business goals.

c:
Sprint Planning: defines work to be completed during upcoming sprint; describes how the team will accomplish it

Daily Scrum: Synchronize activities, create plan for the following day

Sprint Review: Demonstrate completed/incomplete work, gather feedback for future iterations

d: Epic: Large body of work, such as a aspect/functionality of a product
Story: describes a specific user-focused feature

Task: technical step to complete story

Bug: existing error that needs fixing
